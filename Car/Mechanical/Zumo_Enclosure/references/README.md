The user identified the power bank as the gray **AUKEY PB-Y91 Swivel Mini,
35 W, 10000 mAh, dual built-in USB-C cables**. Its published dimensions are
**75.3 x 66.5 x 24.8 mm**. Plug bodies, cable roots, bend space and retention
clearance must be accounted for separately. Manufacturer product values are
reference inputs, not a fit measurement of the user's unit.

The purchase screenshot was used only to identify the power-bank model and
was not copied into the repository. Source images do not establish any new
pin assignment, power connection or chassis hole coordinate.
